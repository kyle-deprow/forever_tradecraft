The guide's path to 300

The BoF Guide plans Cooking from 1 to 300 in 11 steps: 5 recipes to craft and 4 to learn, picked from Forever's own recipe changes at the lowest cost. Each craft ticks off as your skill rises, and its first steps are below.

  1. Train the next rank
  2. Craft Goldthorn Tea until 231
  3. The free guide takes it to 225; the faction packs take it on to 300.
